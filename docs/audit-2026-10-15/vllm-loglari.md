All figures below are copied from public GitHub issues and PRs, read on 2026-10-15. "n/l" means not logged.
"(assumed T)" means the card total is not in that log, so it is taken from another log of the same card (cited in Part 2).
Overhead = requested − weights − KV, where requested = gmu × torch-visible total. vLLM's own "Desired GPU memory utilization is (u, X GiB)" prints the requested figure X directly.

### 1c. Observations

- On recent single-GPU runs, overhead (requested − weights − KV) is about **1.9–3.1 GiB**: rows 1, 2, 15, 18 and 19, all TP1 with up to 64 sequences.
- Larger setups show larger overhead:
  - TP2 to TP4 runs land at about 2.4–4.4 GiB.
  - Big-MoE EP/DP runs carry about 7.7–12 GiB of activation and non-torch memory (NCCL, all2all and DeepEP buffers): rows 5, 7, 11, 16 and 17.
  - The DP16 + EPLB decode in row 20 reaches 24 GiB.
  - Multi-node TP16 reaches 33 GiB in row 8, but that run has a confirmed workspace leak.
- **Before v0.21**, CUDA-graph memory was captured after the KV sizing. Graph memory (1.2–1.9 GiB in rows 9, 13 and 14) then sits outside the gmu budget, which is why old-version overheads look small (0.8–1.1 GiB).
- **From v0.21**, CUDA-graph memory profiling is on by default. The log states the equivalent gmu, so you can back out the graph estimate as (gmu − equivalent) × T.
- **Unified-memory parts break the accounting.** GH200 on v0.17+ (row 9b) and GB10 (row 21, graph estimate −20.50 GiB) produce negative overhead and over-allocate KV.
- **Weights figure:** "Model loading took X GiB" is per rank and already includes the TP/EP shard. On v0.2x it matches "weights" in the free-memory line within about 0.1 GiB.

### ECC on GDDR datacenter and pro cards

- **The 1/16 reservation is confirmed by on/off pairs of nvidia-smi totals:**
  - T4: 16384 → 15360 MiB (exactly 15/16).
  - L40S, A40, RTX A6000 and RTX 6000 Ada: 49140 → 46068 MiB. That is 3072 MiB, equal to 49152 × 1/16, less 12 MiB.
  - L4 (23034) and A10/A10G (23028) are 24576 × 15/16 less 6–12 MiB. Only ECC-on samples were found for these two.
  - An NVIDIA forum post quotes the CUDA Best Practices Guide: with ECC on, GDDR cards lose 6.25% of DRAM to ECC bits ([forum thread](https://forums.developer.nvidia.com/t/nvidia-t4-has-only-15g-of-memory/173099)).
- **Defaults seen in the field:**
  - Datacenter cards ship with ECC on: T4, L4, L40S, A10/A10G, A40 all show "0" in the ECC column on cloud hosts.
  - Workstation RTX A6000 and RTX 6000 Ada ship with ECC off (49140).
  - **RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (GDDR7) reports 97887 MiB whether ECC is on or off**, so it shows no 1/16 loss.
- **HBM cards** (V100, A100, A30, H100 and later) show no capacity loss from ECC. For example, V100 shows 32768 and H100 shows 81559 in both states.
