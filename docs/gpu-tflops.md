# Dense tensor throughput by device

The `tflops` figures in `src/lib/gpus.ts`, used for time to first token and the compute ceiling of large decode batches (`src/lib/perf.ts`). Dense means without structured sparsity: where NVIDIA prints only the sparse figure it is halved. GeForce cards are listed at the FP32-accumulate rate, half the FP16-accumulate one. Collected 2026-10-13 from the vendors' pages and datasheets; a secondary source is named only where no vendor figure exists.

Confidence: **high** — a vendor figure, read directly or halved from a sparse one; **medium** — derived from a vendor figure with an architecture ratio or an assumption; **low** — no tensor figure published, a general GPU figure used instead, or none.
