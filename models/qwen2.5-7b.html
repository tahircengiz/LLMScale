Qwen2.5 7B Instruct: how much VRAM does it need?

Qwen2.5 7B Instruct has 7.62B parameters in 28 layers. It uses grouped-query attention: 28 query heads share 4 key-value heads, so its KV cache is 7× smaller than full multi-head attention would need. It takes up to 32,768 tokens of context; one user at that length needs 1.75 GiB of KV cache at FP16 on top of the weights.

BF16 · 8k context · one user

14.19 GiB of weights, 448 MiB of KV cache and 2.21 GiB of activations and CUDA context. At INT4 the same workload needs 5.13 GiB.

Which single GPUs hold it

  • BF16 (16.84 GiB): RTX 3090 / Ti, RTX 4090, AMD RX 7900 XTX, NVIDIA L4
  • FP8 (9.03 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super
  • INT4 (5.13 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super

At 8k context for one user, the smallest cards first. A discrete card is budgeted at 95% of its memory; a unified-memory device at the share its GPU can address.

Where the numbers come from

Weights are parameters × bytes per parameter. The KV cache is 2 × layers × key-value heads × head dimension × bytes × context × users. For Qwen2.5 7B Instruct that is 56.0 KiB per token at FP16. These are capacity-planning figures: the serving engine — vLLM, TGI, llama.cpp — and its paged-attention efficiency still move the real number.
