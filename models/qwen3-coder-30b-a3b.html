Qwen3 Coder 30B A3B: how much VRAM does it need?

Qwen3 Coder 30B A3B has 30.53B parameters in 48 layers. It uses grouped-query attention: 32 query heads share 4 key-value heads, so its KV cache is 8× smaller than full multi-head attention would need. It is a Mixture-of-Experts model with about 3.30B of its 30.53B parameters active per token: memory has to hold all of them, while each token passes through only the active share. It takes up to 262,144 tokens of context; one user at that length needs 24.00 GiB of KV cache at FP16 on top of the weights.

Where the numbers come from

Weights are parameters × bytes per parameter. The KV cache is 2 × layers × key-value heads × head dimension × bytes × context × users. For Qwen3 Coder 30B A3B that is 96.0 KiB per token at FP16. These are capacity-planning figures: the serving engine — vLLM, TGI, llama.cpp — and its paged-attention efficiency still move the real number.
