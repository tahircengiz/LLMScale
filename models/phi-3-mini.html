Phi-3 mini 4k: how much VRAM does it need?

Phi-3 mini 4k has 3.82B parameters in 32 layers. It uses full multi-head attention: each of its 32 heads keeps its own keys and values, so the KV cache is as large as its head count makes it. It takes up to 4,096 tokens of context; one user at that length needs 1.50 GiB of KV cache at FP16 on top of the weights.

BF16 · 4k context · one user
10.23 GiB

7.12 GiB of weights, 1.50 GiB of KV cache and 1.61 GiB of activations and CUDA context. At INT4 the same workload needs 4.36 GiB.

Which single GPUs hold it

  • BF16 (10.23 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super
  • FP8 (6.31 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super
  • INT4 (4.36 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super

At 4k context for one user, the smallest cards first. A discrete card is budgeted at 95% of its memory; a unified-memory device at the share its GPU can address.

Where the numbers come from

Weights are parameters × bytes per parameter. The KV cache is 2 × layers × key-value heads × head dimension × bytes × context × users. For Phi-3 mini 4k that is 384.0 KiB per token at FP16. These are capacity-planning figures: the serving engine — vLLM, TGI, llama.cpp — and its paged-attention efficiency still move the real number.
