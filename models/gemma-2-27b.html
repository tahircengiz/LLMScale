Gemma 2 27B IT: how much VRAM does it need?

Gemma 2 27B IT has 27.20B parameters in 46 layers. It uses grouped-query attention: 32 query heads share 16 key-value heads, so its KV cache is 2× smaller than full multi-head attention would need. It takes up to 8,192 tokens of context; one user at that length needs 2.88 GiB of KV cache at FP16 on top of the weights. Its repository is gated on Hugging Face, so LLMScale ships its architecture in a bundled database.

Where the numbers come from

Weights are parameters × bytes per parameter. The KV cache is 2 × layers × key-value heads × head dimension × bytes × context × users. For Gemma 2 27B IT that is 368.0 KiB per token at FP16. These are capacity-planning figures: the serving engine — vLLM, TGI, llama.cpp — and its paged-attention efficiency still move the real number.
