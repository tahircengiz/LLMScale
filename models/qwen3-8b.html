Qwen3 8B: how much VRAM does it need?

Qwen3 8B has 8.19B parameters in 36 layers. It uses grouped-query attention: 32 query heads share 8 key-value heads, so its KV cache is 4× smaller than full multi-head attention would need. It takes up to 40,960 tokens of context; one user at that length needs 5.63 GiB of KV cache at FP16 on top of the weights.

  • FP8 (10.38 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super
  • INT4 (6.18 GiB): RTX 3060, RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, RTX 4070 Ti Super, RTX 4080 / Super

At 8k context for one user, the smallest cards first. A discrete card is budgeted at 95% of its memory; a unified-memory device at the share its GPU can address.

Where the numbers come from

Weights are parameters × bytes per parameter. The KV cache is 2 × layers × key-value heads × head dimension × bytes × context × users. For Qwen3 8B that is 144.0 KiB per token at FP16. These are capacity-planning figures: the serving engine — vLLM, TGI, llama.cpp — and its paged-attention efficiency still move the real number.
