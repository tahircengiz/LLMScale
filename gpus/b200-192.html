Which LLMs fit on B200 192GB?

B200 192GB has 192 GB of memory. LLMScale budgets 182.4 GiB of it for a model, leaving 5% for the driver. Its memory bandwidth, 8,000 GB/s, is what limits generation speed once a model fits. Of the 25 popular open models below, 21 fit at BF16, 23 at FP8 and 23 at INT4 with an 8k context for one user.
