Which LLMs fit on RTX A6000?

RTX A6000 has 48 GB of memory. LLMScale budgets 43.73 GiB of it for a model: vLLM's default --gpu-memory-utilization of 0.92 of what the card reports, the rest kept for the CUDA context. Its memory bandwidth, 768 GB/s, is what limits generation speed once a model fits. Of the 31 popular open models below, 16 fit at BF16, 22 at FP8 and 23 at INT4 with an 8k context for one user.
