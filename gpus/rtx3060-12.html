Which LLMs fit on RTX 3060?

RTX 3060 has 12 GB of memory. LLMScale budgets 11.40 GiB of it for a model, leaving 5% for the driver. Its memory bandwidth, 360 GB/s, is what limits generation speed once a model fits. Of the 25 popular open models below, 4 fit at BF16, 9 at FP8 and 12 at INT4 with an 8k context for one user.
